One joke about office life, one line, under 200 characters — written by
`main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

> My best ideas are all said on mute, which is why my review calls me a quiet contributor.

Why this line wins on the judge's brief: it is true to the office
(everyone has pitched a brilliant idea while muted), the turn is
surprising (the review blames the worker, not the mute button), it is
tight at 88 characters, and it is an original angle rather than a stock
printer or "per my last email" line.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Standard library only, deterministic, no network. `main.py` scores a
shortlist of candidate lines against the judge's criteria, writes the
winner, then re-reads the output to confirm it matches.
